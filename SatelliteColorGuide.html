Slider Tips

Greens look like Swamp instead of Forest? Raise the Forest (green) sensitivity slider — it lowers the saturation threshold so darker greens qualify as forest.
Blue sky or haze is classified as Water? Lower the Water (blue) sensitivity slider — it requires higher blue saturation before a pixel is called water.
Mountains coming out as Plains? Raise the Mountain (pale) sensitivity slider — it accepts slightly less-white pixels as mountain/rock.
Image upside-down? Enable Flip image vertically in the options — some satellite exports have south at the top.
Blocky / noisy result? Increase the Sample radius slider. Each hex will average a wider area of pixels, smoothing out noise.
